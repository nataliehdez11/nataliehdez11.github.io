Welcome! I am an Agora Academy Postdoctoral Fellow at the SNF Agora Institute at Johns Hopkins University. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Yale University in May 2026, where I graduated with departmental distinction.

My research examines questions around political representation, public opinion, and persuasion, with a current focus on abortion policy in the post-*Roe v. Wade* era. My current research agenda addresses address three interrelated questions: First, to what extent does state-level policy align with state-level public preferences, and when and why does it diverge? Second, do Americans have meaningful preferences—an essential condition for democratic representation? And third, can life events or external forces change those preferences?

To study these questions, I use large-scale surveys, panel data, and survey and field experiments. My research has been conditionally accepted or published in the *American Political Science Review*, *American Journal of Political Science*, *Public Opinion Quarterly*, *Political Behavior*, and *American Politics Research*.

Before graduate school, I worked at the Boston Consulting Group. I received my B.A. in Political Science and Spanish from the University of Pennsylvania.
